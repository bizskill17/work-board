Workboard v4.18.182
- Monthly Sheet: new columns Previous CL, CL, CL Used, Remaining CL (also in Excel).
  - 1 CL is credited every month from the joining month.
  - CL is used against Absent days (CL Used = smaller of Absent and available CL).
  - Unused CL carries forward: Previous CL is worked out from the last 6 months' attendance.
  - Payable Days = Working Days + Sunday + Holiday + CL Used (Payable Salary follows).
