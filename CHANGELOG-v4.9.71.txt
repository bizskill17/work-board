Business Board v4.9.71

- Added a radio-style In Progress control directly below each task completion checkbox.
- Clicking In Progress opens a compact mandatory progress remark box; no need to open Task Details.
- In Progress remarks are saved with the current user and timestamp and recorded in Activity/History.
- Clicking the In Progress control again on an already In Progress task opens Add Progress Update.
- Completion checkbox remains independent and continues to mark tasks Completed.
