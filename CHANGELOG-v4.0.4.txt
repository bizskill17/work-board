Business Board v4.0.4

- Redesigned Workspace task cards using the selected clean Option 2 layout.
- Task title now gets maximum width and is limited to two lines.
- Only View/Update and More actions remain visible on the card.
- Edit, Pin/Unpin, Move Up and Move Down moved into the More menu.
- Pin indicator appears only when the task is pinned.
- Recurring icon and compact U/H/M/L priority remain beside the title.
- Assignee and due date remain on one compact metadata row.
- Sub Task progress now uses a compact progress bar plus 0/3 style count.
- Reduced card clutter, stronger typography, cleaner white background, and subtle status-colored left border.
