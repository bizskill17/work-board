Workboard v4.14.54

- Added Manager/Owner approval for recorded overtime; unapproved overtime remains unpaid.
- Supports partial overtime approval and prevents approval beyond the remaining recorded overtime.
- Added date-wise manual Extra Hours Credit and Hours Deduction for individual Users.
- Every approval, credit and deduction requires a reason and records the acting Manager/Owner.
- Added an immutable payroll-hours ledger to each User's daily attendance detail.
- Missing OUT, incomplete break and still-working days continue to hold salary; adjustments apply only after attendance becomes complete.
- Monthly Sheet and Excel now include Recorded Overtime, Approved Overtime and Manual +/- Hours.
- Payable hours, payable-day proportion and salary now include approved overtime and manual adjustments.
