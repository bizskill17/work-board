Business Board v4.10.16

- Reworked expanded sub tasks into a compact Todoist-style list.
- Each sub task now uses a small drag handle, checkbox and clean single-line text row.
- Added a simple “+ List item” row at the bottom of the expanded list.
- Clicking “+ List item” turns that row into an inline text field; press Enter to add, or × to cancel.
- Added drag-and-drop reordering directly in the expanded card subtask list.
- Main task completion remains separate; completing all sub tasks does not auto-complete the main task.
