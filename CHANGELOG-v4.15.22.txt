Workboard v4.15.22
- Reopening a completed task no longer asks for Remarks.
- Reopen Task is available only to WordPress Administrators and Workboard Managers.
- Workboard Owners and Employees cannot reopen completed tasks.
- Completed tasks show a static completed check for users who cannot reopen them.
- Added the same Administrator/Manager-only reopen restriction on the backend for both one-time and recurring tasks.
- Completing a task still follows Update Remarks Required when that Work Area setting is enabled.
