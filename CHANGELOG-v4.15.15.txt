Workboard v4.15.15
- Dashboard + Add Task now opens the complete Add Task form immediately.
- Removed the preliminary Workspace / Work Area picker step from the Dashboard + flow.
- The form opens with an available Workspace and Work Area already selected.
- Workspace and Work Area remain visible at the top of the full task form and can be changed there.
- Changing Workspace reloads its available Work Areas.
- Changing Work Area reloads the same Work Area task form context, including defaults and assignees.
- The Dashboard + flow now matches the task form used directly under a Work Area.
