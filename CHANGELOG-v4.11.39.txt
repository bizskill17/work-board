Workboard v4.11.39

- Destination Work Area is recalculated continuously while the board auto-scrolls.
- Vertical card positioning remains active inside the destination Work Area.
- Mobile task drag starts only from the existing task drag handle; normal task-body touch remains available for page scrolling.
