Workboard v4.15.04
- Add Task action sections now stay open once they contain data.
- Sub-tasks stays open when at least one sub-task has text.
- Priority stays open when a priority is selected.
- Labels stays open when one or more labels are selected.
- Notes stays open when Notes contains text.
- A populated action cannot be collapsed accidentally; clear its value first if you want the section to become collapsible again.
- Empty action sections can still be opened and closed normally.
