Business Board v4.3.0
- Removed Work Area count from Workspace KPI chips.
- Mobile header now provides Analysis, Add Work Area, Filter (funnel), and Notification actions.
- Mobile KPI counts stay hidden until Analysis is opened.
- Mobile Work Areas are stacked vertically instead of horizontal left/right scrolling.
- Mobile hides Work Areas that have no Current, Overdue, or Completed tasks.
- Mobile hides empty task sections inside a Work Area.
- Upcoming section and Upcoming KPI are hidden on mobile.
- Desktop Workspace board behavior remains unchanged.
