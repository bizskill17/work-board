Workboard v4.15.07
- Implemented the approved compact Reminder mockup in Add Task.
- Before Due now opens a dedicated card with explicit Number + Unit rows.
- Units support Minutes, Hours, Days and Weeks; Minutes is available when Due Time exists.
- Multiple Before Due reminders can be added using + Add Reminder.
- After Due now opens its own card with explicit Repeat every Number + Unit controls.
- After Due also includes For next and Maximum reminders controls.
- Removed Same Day from the repeat interval unit; Same Day remains available as a duration choice.
- Reminder cards are visually distinct but compact and responsive on mobile.
- Existing reminder backend/save logic is preserved.
