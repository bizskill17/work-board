Business Board v4.9.69

Completed recurring visibility + manual archive
- Daily recurring completed occurrences remain visible for the completion day and automatically leave the operational board the next day.
- Weekly, Fortnightly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly and Custom completed occurrences remain visible in Completed until the Owner archives or deletes them.
- Existing completed non-daily recurring history is surfaced again automatically; no duplicate occurrence is created and original completion timestamps/history are preserved.
- Long-press/right-click Archive on a recurring occurrence archives that occurrence only; it does not stop or archive the recurring series.
