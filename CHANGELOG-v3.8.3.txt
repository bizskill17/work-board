Business Board v3.8.3

- Fixed lean recurring-task calculation so a past scheduled occurrence that has no history row yet is still treated as unresolved.
- The Workspace now always shows the oldest unresolved recurring occurrence first.
- Example: yearly task starting 01/07/2026 and not completed now stays Overdue on 01/07/2026 instead of jumping to 01/07/2027.
- Applies to Daily, Weekly, Fortnightly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly and Custom Interval recurrences.
- Future occurrences remain virtual; no unnecessary rows are pre-created.
