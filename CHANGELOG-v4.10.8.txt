Business Board v4.10.8

- Added per-task notification control while adding a task (enabled by default).
- Assignees receive an in-app notification when newly assigned to a task.
- Task owners receive an in-app notification when task details/status/progress/subtasks are updated.
- Notification messages are also sent by email when Email Notifications is enabled in Settings.
- Existing global "New task assignment" setting is respected.
- Notifications can be disabled for an individual task from the task notification option.
