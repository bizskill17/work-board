Business Board v4.9.66

Recurring incomplete-occurrence policy
- Daily recurring tasks reset operationally each day: previous missed daily occurrences stay in Activity/History but do not carry forward as Overdue on the board.
- Weekly, Fortnightly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly and Custom recurrence now default to "Show overdue until next recurrence".
- Added optional "Keep showing overdue until completed" policy for non-daily recurring tasks.
- When the next recurrence arrives under the default policy, the previous missed occurrence leaves the operational board but remains preserved in History/Activity.
- Under Keep policy, older incomplete occurrences remain Overdue while the fresh scheduled occurrence also appears.
- Added Incomplete Occurrence setting to the full recurring-task form and Repeat quick popover.
- Daily summary counts now follow the same visible-overdue policy.
- Workspace templates/imports preserve the new recurring overdue policy.
