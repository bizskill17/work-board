v4.16.19
- Short Hours and Day Credit now use Attendance Settings > Grace Time (minutes).
- Effective required working minutes = scheduled working minutes minus configured grace minutes.
- If net working minutes meet the grace-adjusted requirement, attendance is credited as 1.00 Day.
- Late arrival continues to use the same configured grace minutes.
- Overtime still starts only after the full scheduled working minutes, so grace does not create overtime.
