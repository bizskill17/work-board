Business Board v4.9.4

- Added User Role field with Owner, Manager and Employee options.
- Existing users are migrated safely to Employee by default.
- Owner and Manager logins can see all application views, including Users, Workspace Templates, Activity Log, Reports and Settings.
- Owner and Manager logins receive full board visibility instead of being restricted only to directly shared workspaces.
- Owner and Manager can perform management actions throughout Business Board.
- Employee logins keep the restricted/shared-item experience.
- User list now includes the Role column.
