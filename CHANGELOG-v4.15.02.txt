Workboard v4.15.02
- Add Task Reminder now stays disabled until BOTH Assignee(s) and Due Date are selected.
- Notify remains disabled until at least one Assignee is selected.
- Made the Add Task modal shorter by collapsing Priority, Labels and Notes under a single More Options control.
- More Options expands only when needed and can be collapsed again with Less Options.
- Sub-tasks remain directly available in the main form for fast task breakdown.
- Existing Priority, Labels multi-select, Add New Label and Notes save logic is preserved.
