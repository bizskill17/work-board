Business Board v3.8.2

Recurring occurrence correction
- Fixed a recurring-task bug where an unresolved past occurrence could be skipped and the Workspace displayed the next future recurrence instead.
- The Workspace now keeps the oldest unresolved occurrence actionable until it is completed, skipped, or otherwise resolved.
- Example: a yearly task due 01/07/2026 and still incomplete remains Overdue on 01/07/2026; 01/07/2027 is not shown as the actionable occurrence yet.
- Applies consistently to Daily, Weekly, Fortnightly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly, and Custom Interval tasks.
- Future recurring occurrences are still calculated virtually; no future task-instance rows are pre-created.
