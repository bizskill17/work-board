Workboard v4.15.30
- Reversed the Attendance Supervisor relationship to the intended model.
- Each employee now has one searchable Supervisor dropdown in Attendance & Payroll.
- Selecting Parashmoni as Aman's Supervisor means Parashmoni can punch Aman's attendance.

- Existing v4.15.28/29 selections are interpreted as the employee's Supervisor for backward compatibility until that User is saved again.
