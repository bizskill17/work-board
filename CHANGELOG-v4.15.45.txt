Workboard v4.15.45
- Made Workspace and Work Area filters dependent on each other in Pending Tasks.
- Selecting one or more Workspaces limits the Work Area dropdown to Work Areas belonging to those Workspaces.
- Selecting one or more Work Areas limits the Workspace dropdown to the corresponding Workspaces.
- Incompatible selections are automatically cleared.
- Added the same two-way Workspace / Work Area dependency in Activity Log.
- Activity Log Workspace and Work Area options are based on actual Activity Log records.
- Searchability and existing filter behavior remain unchanged.
