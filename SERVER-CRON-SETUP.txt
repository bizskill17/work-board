Workboard Scheduled Reminder Cron
=================================

To send reminders even when nobody opens Workboard, configure a real server cron in Hostinger to call WordPress cron every 5 minutes.

Recommended command:

curl -fsS "https://board.bizskilledu.com/wp-cron.php?doing_wp_cron" >/dev/null 2>&1

Schedule: every 5 minutes (*/5 * * * *)

Alternative if curl is not available:

wget -q -O - "https://board.bizskilledu.com/wp-cron.php?doing_wp_cron" >/dev/null 2>&1

Workboard v4.14.12 automatically keeps its bb_task_reminder_tick event scheduled every 5 minutes and records the last reminder check time.
