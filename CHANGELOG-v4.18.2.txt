WorkBoard v4.18.2

- DocSpace: added document upload to Add/Edit Document Movement.
- Supported document uploads: PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP, DOC, DOCX, XLS and XLSX up to 10 MB.
- Existing uploaded document can be viewed from the form and DocSpace Register.
- Replacing a document removes the previous attachment; deleting a DocSpace entry removes its uploaded document.
- CallSpace and DocSpace Settings module switches now default to OFF/hidden for new installations/sites.
- Existing sites keep their saved Show/Hide selection.
