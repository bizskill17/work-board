Workboard v4.11.36

- Simplified active task bifurcation into four mutually exclusive sections:
  Simple: tasks with no due date.
  Overdue: tasks with a due date before today.
  Due: tasks due today or later within the current calendar month.
  Upcoming: tasks due after the current calendar month.
- Completed tasks remain in the separate Completed section.
- Focus view reflects the same Simple / Overdue / Due / Upcoming classification.
- Task movement between Work Areas preserves the task's date-based section.
