Workboard v4.12.40
- Added a Copy icon immediately after Flash on Task Cards.
- Clicking Copy creates a new duplicate task directly below the source task.
- Copies task details, Owners/Assignees, recurrence settings, labels, reminders and Sub Task text/assignments.
- Completion/history are not copied; the duplicate is a fresh task.
- Added the same Copy facility to Note Cards.
- Note Copy appears after Move and creates the same Note directly below the source Note.
