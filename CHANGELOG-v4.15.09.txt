Workboard v4.15.09
- Replaced the separate Before Due / After Due reminder switches with Google Calendar-style reminder rows.
- Each reminder row now contains Number + Unit + Before Due/After Due.
- Users can add multiple independent reminder rules with + Add Reminder.
- Example rules: 30 Minutes Before Due, 1 Day Before Due, 2 Hours After Due.
- When at least one After Due rule exists, an optional Repeat after due section becomes available.
- Repeat after due supports Number + Minutes/Hours/Days/Weeks + duration.
- Reminder creation remains disabled until both Assignee(s) and Due Date are selected.
- Existing backend Before/After reminder fields are populated for compatibility.
