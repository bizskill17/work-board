WorkBoard v4.18.45
- DocSpace upload optimisation.
- Increased DocSpace source upload validation limit from 10 MB to 20 MB (subject to PHP/server limits).
- JPG/PNG/WEBP attachments are automatically resized to a maximum 2000 px long edge and recompressed at quality 80 after upload.
- PDF attachments receive best-effort Ghostscript optimisation when available; digitally signed PDFs are never modified.
- Word and Excel files remain untouched to avoid document corruption.
- DocSpace Save button shows “Uploading & Optimizing…” while the upload is processed.
- Optimisation keeps the same WordPress attachment and URL; replacement uploads continue to remove the previous attachment.
