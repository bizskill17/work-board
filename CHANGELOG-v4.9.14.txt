Business Board v4.9.14

- Filter dropdown menus are now fully opaque white and render above Work Area theme borders, preventing color bleed-through.
- Workspace search now includes Work Area names.
- When searching, Work Areas with no matching Work Area name and no matching task/assignee data are hidden.
- If the Work Area name itself matches the search, the Work Area is shown with its tasks.
- Work Area filter options continue to list all Work Areas.
