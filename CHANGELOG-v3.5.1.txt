Business Board v3.5.1
=======================

Workspace filter layout fix
- Work Area, Priority and Assignee filters now remain on one compact horizontal row on desktop.
- Removed the premature 1100px stacking breakpoint that could trigger on desktop browsers with display scaling/zoom.
- Desktop layout is explicitly protected from global filter-field rules.
- Narrow tablet layouts can horizontally scroll the compact filter controls instead of creating three full-width rows.
- Mobile behaviour remains unchanged.
