Version 4.14.23
- Added configurable Maximum Accepted Attendance GPS Accuracy in Attendance Settings.
- Default accuracy limit is 150 metres.
- Preset suggestions: 50, 100, 150 and 250 metres; custom values are supported.
- Mandatory location mode now validates against the configured accuracy limit on both browser and server.
- GPS capture accepts the first reading at or better than the configured threshold and otherwise keeps trying briefly for a better reading.
