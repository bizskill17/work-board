Workboard v4.11.5
- Added Workspace Archive view.
- Active Workspace cards now support Edit, Archive, and Move to Trash.
- Archived Workspaces can be Unarchived or moved to Trash.
- Trashed Workspaces can be Restored or Deleted Permanently.
- Restore returns a Workspace to its state before Trash (active/inactive/archived).
- Archived Workspace tasks are hidden from normal operational boards and reports.
