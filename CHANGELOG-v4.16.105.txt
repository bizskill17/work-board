Workboard v4.16.105

- Daily Activity form now uses separate From and To timestamp fields.
- Removed manual Time Spent input from the form.
- Activity duration is calculated automatically from From and To.
- Added validation that To must be later than From and duration cannot exceed 24 hours.
- Daily Activity table now shows both From and To timestamps.
- Existing Time Sheet entries remain compatible; older entries without a To timestamp continue to display.
