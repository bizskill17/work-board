Workboard v4.15.03
- Replaced More Options with a compact Actions section in the Add Task modal.
- Actions now include Sub-tasks, Priority, Labels and Notes.
- Each action opens only its own section and can be collapsed independently.
- Multiple action sections can stay open together when needed.
- Sub-tasks are now hidden by default and open only when the Sub-tasks action is clicked.
- Action buttons show a compact status after values are entered: sub-task count, selected priority, label count and Notes checkmark.
- Reminder remains disabled until both Assignee(s) and Due Date are selected.
