Workboard v4.15.74

- Restored Share option for Notespace and MathSpace while keeping Note Area and Math Area sharing.
- Notespace/MathSpace Share is available from the space card and from inside the opened space.
- Space-level sharing is inherited by all child Areas.
- Owners can manage the space and its child Areas; Assignees can view and update existing content.
- Area-level ADD / EDIT / DELETE / VIEW permissions continue to work independently for direct Area shares.
- Create New User remains available only from Master > Users.
