Business Board v4.7.2

- Daily recurring tasks no longer appear in Upcoming.
- A daily recurring task is shown only when its occurrence is due today (Current), overdue, or completed.
- After today's daily occurrence is completed, tomorrow's occurrence remains hidden until tomorrow.
- Weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly, yearly, custom-interval, and one-time future tasks continue to use Upcoming / More Upcoming rules.
- Upcoming KPI counts also exclude hidden future daily occurrences.
