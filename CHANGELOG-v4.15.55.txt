Workboard v4.15.55
- Added Share option to Notespace.
- Added Share option to MathSpace.
- Notespace and MathSpace sharing use the same Owner / Assignee model as Workspace.
- Owner: Edit + Update + Share.
- Assignee: View + Update.
- Added Share buttons on Notespace and MathSpace cards and inside their active view toolbars.
- MathSpace create/edit form now includes MathSpace Owners & Assignees.
- MathSpace sharing is inherited by all Math Areas inside that MathSpace.
- Direct Math Area sharing continues to work for narrower access.
- Existing legacy MathSpaces without top-level sharing remain visible under the prior behavior to avoid hiding old data.
- MathSpace assignments are now included in application state and deleted when a MathSpace is deleted.
