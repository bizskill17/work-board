Business Board v4.5.0

Quick Add improvements
- Added two instant task actions in every Work Area header:
  - ↻+ adds Daily Recurring tasks
  - + adds One-Time tasks
- One-Time Quick Add keeps Due Date blank by default.
- Quick Add stays open after each save so tasks can be entered one after another.
- The selected Quick Add mode remains active for the batch session.
- Quick options are hidden behind the gear icon.
- Daily/recurring quick options: Frequency, Priority, Due Time, Assignee.
- One-Time quick options: Due Date, Priority, Due Time, Assignee.
- Quick option values are remembered during the current batch-entry session.
- Detailed fields such as Sub Tasks, Owners, Instructions, Attachments and advanced recurrence remain in Edit Task.
