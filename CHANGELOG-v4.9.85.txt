Business Board v4.9.85

- Rebuilt task visibility for Priority filtering using strict DOM visibility rather than class-only hiding.
- Priority filter now forcibly removes non-matching cards from Current, Overdue, Upcoming and Completed sections.
- Empty sections and Work Areas collapse immediately after filtering.
- Priority checkbox changes are handled on both input and change events for more reliable browser behavior.
- Combined Work Area, Assignee and Overdue filtering remains supported.
