Workboard v4.15.27
- Added Attendance > Supervisor permission to the User form.
- Supervisor is an attendance-only permission and does not grant Manager/Owner access elsewhere in Workboard.
- An Attendance Supervisor can punch today's attendance for other active Users whose Attendance is enabled.
- Dashboard Punch Attendance opens Team Attendance for Supervisors.
- AttendSpace shows attendance-enabled team members to Supervisors with live IN/OUT and configured Out/In combo punch buttons.
- Supervisors cannot use Manager-only past attendance corrections, delete attendance, holiday management, reports, settings or payroll controls.
- Attendance event audit continues to store the actual employee being punched and the User who performed the punch.
- Punch History now shows “Punch · by <Supervisor>” when another User made the punch.
- Added employees.attendance_supervisor database field; DB schema migration runs once on this version.
