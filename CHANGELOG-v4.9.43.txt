Business Board v4.9.43

- Changed Sub Tasks to a Google Keep-style interaction: existing items plus a visible + Add Sub Task row.
- Adding a sub task no longer requires Enter; typing and tapping elsewhere auto-saves. Enter remains an optional shortcut and opens the next blank sub-task row.
- Removed instructional Press Enter text and Save-style interaction from inline sub-task entry.
- Changed Labels to use Search Labels plus a visible + Add Label action.
- New labels auto-save on blur/tap-away; Enter remains optional.
- Added compact cancel × controls for unfinished new sub-task/label rows.
