Version 4.14.21
- Improved attendance GPS capture accuracy for live IN/OUT punches.
- Workboard now samples multiple high-accuracy browser/device readings for up to 15 seconds and saves the best reading instead of immediately accepting the first GPS result.
- Stops early when accuracy reaches approximately 35 metres or better.
- Existing reverse geocoding continues to use the exact stored latitude/longitude; readable address remains a display aid.
- If the device cannot provide a stronger GPS fix, the best available reading is still used (or attendance is blocked when location is mandatory and no reading is available).
