Version 4.13.76
- Fixed recurring schedule anchoring: task creation date can no longer become a false recurrence date.
- Yearly/Monthly/Weekly/etc. remain anchored to the configured Start Date.
- Repairs unresolved legacy phantom recurrence rows that are off-schedule.
- Recurring task deletion now distinguishes Delete This Occurrence from Delete Entire Recurring Series.
- Deleting one occurrence creates a recurrence exception so it is not recreated on refresh.
- Bulk deletion of a recurring occurrence now also preserves the recurring series.
