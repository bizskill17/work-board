Version 4.14.03
- Added AFTER DUE reminders that repeat only while a task remains incomplete.
- Repeat interval supports Minutes, Hours, Days and Weeks.
- Added Active Hours window plus 24 Hours mode.
- Added optional Maximum Duration and Maximum Reminders limits; 0 means unlimited/until completed.
- After-due reminders continue on future days and stop automatically on completion.
- Reminder delivery remains occurrence-specific for recurring tasks and uses the existing Task Reminder WhatsApp template.
- Archived/deleted Work Areas and Workspaces are excluded from reminder processing.
