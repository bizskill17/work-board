Business Board v4.9.47

- Labels now use the same one-tap continuous-add flow as Sub Tasks.
- Tapping + Add Label while text is entered saves/selects that label and immediately opens the next blank label row.
- Enter remains an optional shortcut for the same behavior.
- Duplicate labels are prevented case-insensitively and with normalized whitespace.
- Existing duplicate label choices are deduplicated in the inline label picker.
