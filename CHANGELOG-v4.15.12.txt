Workboard v4.15.12
- Added Send During controls for Repeat After Due reminders.
- Repeat After Due can now run during 24 Hours, Office Hours, or Custom Hours.
- 24 Hours sets the active window to 00:00–23:59.
- Office Hours uses the organization office-hours settings when available; fallback is 09:30–19:00.
- Custom Hours reveals From and To time fields.
- These send-window controls apply only to repeating after-due reminders.
- Individual Before Due and individual After Due reminder rules continue to fire at their calculated exact time.
