Business Board v4.9.33
- Added drag-and-drop movement of task cards between Work Areas using the existing six-dot grip.
- Drag within the same Work Area still reorders tasks vertically.
- Destination Work Area receives a green outline while hovered during a cross-area drag.
- Dropped task keeps its task data and slug; only its Work Area and manual order change.
- Source/destination counts and filtered board state refresh immediately after a successful move.
- Supports Current, Overdue, Upcoming and Completed task buckets, including empty destination buckets.
