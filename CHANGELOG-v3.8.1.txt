Business Board v3.8.1

Recurring Workspace visibility fix
- Removed the 7-day display-window limitation for recurring tasks on Workspace boards.
- Every active recurring task now contributes its next actionable occurrence to its Workspace, regardless of how far in the future it is.
- Daily, weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly, yearly and custom-interval tasks are handled consistently.
- Completed recurring occurrences are skipped when calculating the next occurrence.
- Existing overdue/missed occurrences remain visible.
- Recurring occurrences completed today remain visible in Completed while the next occurrence can also appear as Upcoming.
- Future recurrence rows are still calculated virtually and are not pre-created in the database.
