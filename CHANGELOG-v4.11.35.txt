Workboard v4.11.35

- Added a dedicated Focus view to the frontend navigation.
- Focus view shows active Focus-marked tasks across all accessible Workspaces.
- Each Workspace is displayed as a frontend column (Work Area-style presentation only); database hierarchy is unchanged.
- Each Focus card shows its real Work Area for context.
- Removing Focus from a task while in Focus view removes it immediately from the view.
- Focus view cards cannot be dragged between the virtual Workspace columns.
