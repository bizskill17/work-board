WorkBoard v4.18.65

- Attendance summary image uses Arial-compatible TrueType rendering when available, with Arial font lookup and Liberation Sans fallback.
- Morning 11:00 AM summary: Total, Present, Absent, Late and On Leave.
- Late is calculated per employee using that employee's Start Time plus configured Grace Minutes.
- Evening 8:00 PM summary: Total, Present, Absent, Early, Less Working Hours and On Leave.
- Early is calculated per employee using that employee's End Time minus configured Grace Minutes.
- Less Working Hours follows each employee's Start/End Time and also allows configured Grace Minutes.
- Approved leave is excluded from Absent and counted separately as On Leave.
