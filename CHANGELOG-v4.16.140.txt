Workboard v4.16.140
- Added drag-and-drop reordering for Note Areas inside a Notespace, matching Work Area movement.
- Desktop: drag the Note Area grip left/right.
- Mobile/tablet: long-press the grip, then drag; horizontal auto-scroll works near the edges.
- Note Cards remain inside their Note Area while the Area moves.
- Note Area order is saved to sort_order and persists after refresh/login.
- Reordering is disabled while Notespace search is active to avoid partial-order conflicts.
