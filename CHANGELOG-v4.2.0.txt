Business Board v4.2.0

- Workspaces list redesigned to show Workspace name only.
- Removed Workspace metadata, Work Area names/counts, Department/status/score columns from the Workspace picker.
- Workspace names are clickable cards that open the selected Workspace.
- Added restrained neon accent system across active navigation, primary buttons, focused controls, Workspace cards and task status accents.
- Neon remains subtle on a light SaaS-style interface.
- Mobile Workspaces view uses a simple one-column name list with compact Import/Add controls.
