WorkBoard v4.18.75

- Added duplicate-name validation for Notespace, Note Area, MathSpace and Math Area.
- Notespace and MathSpace names are unique within their respective modules.
- Note Area names are unique within the selected Notespace.
- Math Area names are unique within the selected MathSpace.
- Duplicate checks ignore letter case, leading/trailing spaces and repeated internal spaces.
- Validation runs in both the frontend and server/API.
- Edit mode excludes the current record from duplicate checks.
- Duplicate validation keeps the form open and focuses the Name field.
- Existing duplicate records are not automatically modified or deleted.
