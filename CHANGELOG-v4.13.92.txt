Version 4.13.92

Attendance / Payroll
- Added Employee "Claim Weekly Off" action in AttendSpace for eligible current/past dates.
- Weekly Off claims require Manager/Owner approval before affecting attendance.
- Manager/Owner can Approve or Reject pending Weekly Off claims directly in AttendSpace.
- Approved claims count as Weekly Off and do not count as Absent.
- Rejected claims can be submitted again.
- Added Sunday as a separate paid-day dimension only when Sunday is not already covered by Weekly Off.
- Monthly Payable Days now follows: Present + (Half Day × 0.5) + Holiday + Weekly Off + eligible Sunday.
- Holiday/Weekly Off/Sunday can coexist with worked attendance. Example: full-day work on a Holiday counts Present = 1 and Holiday = 1.
- Late full-day attendance is included in Present while remaining separately visible under Late.
- Attendance Register status filtering supports overlapping Present / Holiday / Weekly Off / Sunday conditions.

Safety
- Holiday dates cannot be claimed as Weekly Off through the employee claim action.
- Future dates cannot be claimed.
- Existing Attendance, MathSpaces, Notes, Tasks and other modules are preserved.
