Workboard v4.11.83

- Added a drag handle to Workspaces shown under "Without Folder" in Folder view.
- Administrators, Owners and Managers can drag a standalone Workspace directly onto a Folder card.
- Folder cards highlight while they are valid drop targets.
- Dropping a Workspace adds it to the selected Folder and preserves all Workspace data and permissions.
- Compatible with the multi-Folder relationship introduced in v4.11.82.
