v4.16.15
- Added Attendance > Leave for employees to submit leave requests with Name, From Date, To Date and Reason.
- Added Attendance > Pending Leave Approval for Supervisors, Managers, Owners and Administrators.
- Pending leave can be updated to Approved or Rejected with approval remark; rejected leave requires a remark.
- Added bb_attendance_leaves table with automatic targeted migration.
- Approved leave appears as Leave in attendance status; if punches exist, attendance status keeps the punch result and also indicates Leave.
- Attendance Register status filter includes Leave.
- Monthly Sheet includes Leave Days.
- Leave visibility respects self, Supervisor and View Attendance access.
