Business Board v3.7.0
- Simplified Create Task form when launched from a Work Area.
- Workspace and Work Area are locked to the context and no longer shown as editable fields.
- Assignee defaults automatically to the Work Area Head and is hidden in contextual task creation.
- Backend also falls back to the Work Area Head when no assignee is supplied.
- Removed Task Summary side panel, Good Task Design box, lean-storage note, eyebrow text and other explanatory clutter.
- Reduced form padding and made the task form a cleaner single-column workflow.
- General task creation/editing outside a Work Area keeps Workspace/Work Area/Assignee controls.
