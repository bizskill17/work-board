WorkBoard v4.18.42

- Time Sheet Remarks cells now show a compact two-line preview by default.
- A down arrow is shown only when the Remarks text exceeds the two-line preview.
- Clicking the arrow expands the full Remarks for that row; clicking again collapses it.
- Line breaks and entered formatting are preserved.
- Excel export continues to use the full Remarks text.
- Works on desktop and mobile without an internal Remarks scrollbar.
