Business Board v4.0.1
- Adds Share on View / Update Task for effective Owners with Edit rights only.
- Share supports multiple direct Owners and Assignees with View, Edit, or View + Edit rights.
- Inherited Workspace / Work Area access is shown read-only and remains intact.
- Task sharing changes are logged in Activity Log.
- Compacts the View / Update Task screen: metadata strip, compact people section, latest remark preview.
- Hides empty Sub Task / Remark summary cards and combines attachments/proof into one operational section.
- Keeps Sub Task checkboxes, remarks, proof, and latest-first activity timeline in the execution view.
