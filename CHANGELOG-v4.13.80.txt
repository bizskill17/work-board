Version 4.13.80
- Normal task edits no longer send the Task Updated notification/WhatsApp message to Task Owners.
- Inline task field edits are also silent.
- Changing task Owners/Assignees does not send Task Updated to Owners.
- Newly-added Assignees still receive the Task Assigned message.
- Progress remarks, stage changes, sub-task activity, completion/reopen and other operational updates keep their existing notification logic.
